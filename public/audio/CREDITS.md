# French pronunciation recordings

These MP3 files are synthetic readings of Motamot’s vocabulary and examples, generated locally with the French feminine voice `ff_siwis`, speed 1.0, using Kokoro v1.0. They are newly generated speech, not excerpts from the source dataset and not recordings by a person reading Motamot’s lessons.

- **Model:** [Kokoro-82M by hexgrad and contributors](https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M), published with Apache-2.0 licensed weights. [Voice details](https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M/blob/main/VOICES.md).
- **Inference tool:** [kokoro-onnx by thewh1teagle](https://github.com/thewh1teagle/kokoro-onnx), MIT license; version 0.6.1, model-files-v1.1 export.
- **Training-data attribution:** Yamagishi, Junichi; Honnet, Pierre-Edouard; Garner, Philip; Lazaridis, Alexandros (2017), *The SIWIS French Speech Synthesis Database, 2016*. University of Edinburgh, School of Informatics, Centre for Speech Technology Research. [Dataset](https://doi.org/10.7488/ds/1705), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Kokoro identifies SIWIS as part of its French training data.

The model, voice weights and generation dependencies are not distributed with this app. The learner’s browser receives only the finished 24 kHz mono MP3 recordings. Source attribution does not imply endorsement of Motamot.
